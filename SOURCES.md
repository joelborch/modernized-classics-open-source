# Source contribution guidelines

When adding or revising a book, record the exact source URL, edition, translator,
editor and publication year in the contribution notes. Keep the input hash and
selection record with the local pipeline workspace.

Select source material you have permission to redistribute. Review translations,
introductions, annotations, illustrations and other editorial material separately
from the original work. Document the cover creator and applicable license.

Keep input EPUBs, archives and pipeline working files outside Git. Follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the review and submission workflow.
