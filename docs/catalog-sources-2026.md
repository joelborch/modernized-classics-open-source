# Catalog source editions for the 2026 refresh

These are the source selections for the Opus 5.5 catalog refresh. This inventory records source provenance and scope; it does not certify that every adaptation has completed review. Source-file hashes, selection manifests, model receipts, scan corrections and independent reviews remain in the local pipeline workspace, outside Git.

## Selected content

Each adaptation covers the documented selection below, including any abridgment already present in the selected historical edition or transcription. Publisher wrappers, duplicate contents and web navigation are excluded. The adaptation policy omits citation markers, citation-only references and obsolete page cross-references while retaining substantive notes and their association with the relevant argument. Per-title selections below distinguish additional historical material from the author’s main text.

- **The Art of War:** All 13 chapters and the historical preface, six introduction sections, bibliography, commentary and notes available in Gutenberg 132. Bob Sutton’s English transcription omits Chinese text and abridges or paraphrases some of Giles’s scholarly material; it is not the complete bilingual 1910 edition available as [Gutenberg 66706](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/66706). The existing catalog already used this reduced edition. Historical translator commentary remains distinct from Sun Tzu’s text through bracket conventions.
- **As a Man Thinketh:** Opening epigraph, author foreword and all seven chapters, retaining verse quotations.
- **Civil Disobedience and Other Essays:** Five complete essays in canonical order: Civil Disobedience; Slavery in Massachusetts; A Plea for Captain John Brown; Walking; Life Without Principle. Gutenberg source reused for first essay; four additional complete Standard Ebooks article files from immutable commit. Historical publication bridgeheads and verse retained.
- **Common Sense:** Opening Thomson epigraph; full Introduction, four main sections, Appendix, complete address to Quakers and source notes through FINIS.
- **The Communist Manifesto and Das Kapital:** The four sections of the Manifesto in Samuel Moore’s 1888 English version, plus all 24 chapters, the editor’s preface and supplement of Julian Borchardt’s The People’s Marx, translated by Stephen L. Trask (International Bookshops, 1921). This is the complete historical abridgement of Capital, not its three unabridged volumes. The abridgement was checked against the Toronto and Sciences Po scans; two original numerical printing anomalies are retained and documented in the local source record. The unrelated Rosa Luxemburg appendix from the secondary transcription site is excluded.
- **The Consolation of Philosophy:** Complete five-book Boethius dialogue and all 39 verse songs; excludes James preface/proem, summaries, epilogue, quotation reference list, 19 translator notes, frontmatter and publisher/Gutenberg material. Prose-embedded verse quotations and dialogue retained.
- **The Critique of Pure Reason:** Complete Meiklejohn translation: author prefaces to 1781 and 1787 editions, Introduction, Doctrine of Elements and Doctrine of Method with all component sections and source notes.
- **The Economic Consequences of the Peace:** Author preface and full seven chapters, all source tables and footnotes. Preserve numeric values and table relationships.
- **The Enchiridion and Fragments:** George Long translation dated 1877 by the Standard Ebooks colophon. All 53 teachings and 181 fragments retained; Latin fragments 179–181 retain the original text alongside English translations. Long introduction and disputed-authorship labels retained and marked. Endnote markers removed, notes excluded. Verse quotations retain line breaks.
- **An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding:** Complete sections I–XII with all author footnotes and historical Selby-Bigge index, from 1902 source representing 1777 text. All prior Millican/Oxford-derived prose excluded.
- **How to Get What You Want:** The five complete Wattles chapters, taken from PDF pages 4–24 of the 2014 Brocker republication of the 1907 work. Modern publisher material, the added Nautilus introduction, running headers and page footers are excluded. The source is a PDF transcription, not an original EPUB.
- **The Interpretation of Dreams:** Full A. A. Brill 1913 translation: introductory remarks, second- and third-edition author prefaces, translator preface, chapters I–VII, VIII Literary Index, general index and all source footnotes.
- **Leviathan:** Dedication and introduction; all four parts and 47 chapters; A Review and Conclusion. Historical tables/notes retained and all paragraph sequences reconciled to selected source blocks.
- **Meditations:** Complete George Long historical edition: Long biographical sketch and philosophy essay, all 12 books, translator notes, index of terms and general index. Introductory translator material remains identified by its source headings.
- **The Nicomachean Ethics:** W. D. Ross’s 1925 translation, verified through the scanned 1931 impression: all ten books, the historical preface and substantive notes. The later 1953 introduction and 1954 revisions are excluded. Greek quotations, verse and note associations are retained.
- **On Liberty, Utilitarianism, and Other Essays:** Four complete works: On Liberty (five chapters, dedication, epigraph and substantive notes); Utilitarianism (1879 seventh edition, five chapters and notes); Considerations on Representative Government (preface, 18 chapters and notes); and The Subjection of Women (1869, four chapters and notes). The Courtney 1901 editorial introduction is excluded.
- **On the Origin of Species:** Complete sixth edition: three opening epigraphs, Historical Sketch, Introduction, chapters I–XV, scientific glossary and historical index.
- **The Problems of Philosophy:** Complete author preface, chapters I–XV and author bibliographical note.
- **Second Treatise of Government:** Locke author preface plus full Second Treatise chapters I–XIX, sections 1–243 and original footnotes.
- **Self-Reliance:** Self-Reliance only; all essay prose and all three epigraphs; other Essays First Series excluded.
- **The Game of Life and How to Play It:** Complete ten author chapters of Gutenberg 74878, original 1925 text; wrapper, contents, post-book advertisement and transcriber notes excluded. Page-number anchors removed without deleting their tails. Quotations retained within chapter text, including seven source poetry containers with line/stanza boundaries.
- **The Prince:** Full dedication and chapters I–XXVI selected by semantic anchor IDs. Introductory biography, contents, unrelated Valentino/Castruccio works and Gutenberg license/wrapper excluded. All 48 explicitly classed translator/editorial footnotes and their reference markers excluded; author text and embedded quotations retained. Two verse blocks retain line/stanza layout and Dacre attribution.
- **The Social Contract & Discourses:** Complete 1920 Cole edition: historical translator introduction, note on books/bibliography, Social Contract four books and 48 chapters, Arts and Sciences discourse, Inequality discourse with dedication/preface/appendix, Political Economy discourse. Retain all historical notes and quotations; exclude duplicated contents, title imprint and Gutenberg wrapper. Current catalog begins with Cole's introduction, so retained with explicit translator attribution.
- **Thus Spoke Zarathustra:** Thomas Common translation: all four parts, the prologue and all 80 numbered discourses through The Sign. Excludes the Förster-Nietzsche and Ludovici editorial apparatus.
- **Varieties of Religious Experience:** James dedication and preface, all 20 lectures, postscript, historical index and all 363 source footnotes.
- **A Vindication of the Rights of Woman:** Author dedication to Talleyrand, Introduction, all 13 chapters including notes and subsections.
- **Walden:** Opening author epigraph and all 18 chapters, with verse quotations and original tables.
- **The Wealth of Nations:** Introduction and Plan of the Work; all five books and 32 chapters, retaining historical tables and embedded notes. Paragraph sequence reconciled to selected source blocks.

## Covers and content terms

The refresh reuses nine project covers and adds nineteen new covers. The nineteen new covers and five of the reused covers have local records identifying the built-in `image_gen` tool and binding the resulting image hashes. The other four reused covers have stored project image-generation prompts and image hashes, but no separate execution receipt in the retained records. Cover files are checked for image integrity, title and author spelling, layout and legibility.

The repository’s existing [content license](../CONTENT-LICENSE.md) offers CC BY 4.0 for project-created adaptations and covers only to the extent Modernized Classics holds rights it can license. This inventory does not expand that offer or assert new rights in historical translations, illustrations or third-party material. No publisher cover is selected as a new cover source.
