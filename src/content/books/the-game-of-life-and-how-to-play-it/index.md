## Chapter 1: The Game

Most people see life as a constant battle. But life isn't a battle—it's a **game**.

However, you can't play this game successfully unless you understand the spiritual laws that govern it. The rules of the game are laid out with amazing clarity in religious texts like the Old and New Testaments. Jesus Christ taught that life is a great game of **Giving and Receiving**.

***

### The Main Rule: You Get What You Give

A core rule of the game is: **"You reap what you sow."**

This means that whatever you send out into the world, through your words or actions, eventually comes back to you. What you give is what you get.

* If you give love, you will receive love.
* If you give hate, you will receive hate.
* If you criticize others, you will be criticized.
* If you lie, you will be lied to.
* If you cheat, you will be cheated.

This law is a fundamental part of how the game of life works.

***

### The Power of Your Imagination

We are also taught that our imagination plays a leading role in the game of life. A proverb says to guard your heart (or your imagination) carefully, because your entire life flows from it.

This means that whatever you imagine in your mind, sooner or later, shows up in your real life.

I knew a man who was terrified of getting a specific, very rare disease. He thought about it and pictured it so constantly that the disease eventually appeared in his body. He died because of his own distorted imagination.

This shows us that to play the game of life well, we must train our imagination. If you train your mind to imagine only good things, you will bring those things into your life. You can attract everything you truly desire: health, wealth, love, good friends, and the chance to express your true self.

The imagination has been called the **"Scissors of the Mind."** Every day, it is cutting out the pictures you hold in your thoughts. Sooner or later, you will meet these creations in the outside world.

***

### Know Yourself: The Three Parts of Your Mind

To train your imagination, you first need to understand how your mind works. The ancient Greeks taught: **"Know Thyself."**

Your mind has three main parts: the **subconscious**, the **conscious**, and the **superconscious**.

1.  **The Subconscious Mind:** This part of your mind is pure power, but it has no direction of its own. It's like raw electricity or steam. It does whatever it's told to do and follows instructions without question. Anything you feel deeply or picture clearly gets impressed upon your subconscious mind, which then works to make it happen in every tiny detail.

    * **For example:** A woman I knew spent her childhood pretending to be a widow. She would dress up in black clothes and a long veil, and the adults around her thought it was cute and funny. When she grew up, she married a man she loved deeply. But after a short time, he died. She then spent many years wearing black, just as she had imagined. The picture of herself as a widow had been so deeply impressed on her subconscious mind that it eventually came true, causing great heartbreak.

2.  **The Conscious Mind:** This is your everyday, human mind. It sees life as it appears on the surface—full of problems like death, disaster, sickness, and poverty. The conscious mind is what gives orders to the subconscious.

3.  **The Superconscious Mind:** This is the God Mind, or Higher Self, that exists within every person. It is a realm of perfect ideas. It holds the **"Divine Design"** for your life—the perfect, unique plan meant just for you. There is a place you are meant to fill that no one else can, and something you are meant to do that no one else can.

This perfect plan often flashes into your conscious mind as an amazing idea that feels "too good to be true." In reality, it's your true destiny, sent to you from the Infinite Intelligence within yourself.

***

### Finding Your True Path

Many people are unaware of their true destinies. They spend their lives striving for things or situations that don't truly belong to them. Even if they get what they want, it only brings them failure and unhappiness.

* **For example:** A woman came to me, deeply in love with a certain man. She asked me to use prayer to make him marry her. I told her that doing so would violate spiritual law. Instead, I said I would pray for the *right* man to come into her life—the "divine selection" who was meant for her. I added, "If this man is the right one, you can't lose him. If he isn't, you'll receive someone who is just as wonderful, or even better."

She continued to see the man, but their friendship never progressed. One night, she told me, "You know, for the past week, he hasn't seemed so special to me anymore." I suggested that perhaps he wasn't her divine selection.

Soon after, she met a new man who fell in love with her instantly. He told her she was his ideal woman and said all the things she had always wished the first man would say. She fell in love with him and completely lost interest in the other man.

This shows the **law of substitution**. When we replace a wrong idea with the right one, there is no loss or sacrifice involved. Jesus taught that if you seek the kingdom of God first—which is the realm of right ideas within you—then everything else you need will be given to you.

***

### Your Words Have Power

Jesus also taught that your words play a huge part in the game of life. He said, **"By your words you are justified, and by your words you are condemned."**

Many people have brought disaster into their lives through careless, idle words.

* **For example:** A woman asked me why her life was now full of poverty and limitation. She told me that she used to have a beautiful home filled with nice things, but she often got tired of managing it all. She used to say, "I'm so sick of all this stuff! I wish I lived in a trunk." She told me, "Today, I am living in that trunk." She had literally spoken herself into a tiny apartment.

The subconscious mind has no sense of humor. People often joke themselves into unhappy situations.

* **Another example:** A very wealthy woman constantly joked about "getting ready for the poorhouse." Within a few years, she had lost nearly all her money. She had impressed her subconscious mind with a picture of lack, and it delivered.

***

### How to Reverse Bad Fortune

Thankfully, the law works both ways. A situation of lack can be changed into one of plenty.

* **For example:** A woman came to me one hot summer day, feeling completely worn out and discouraged. She told me she had only eight dollars to her name. I said, "Good! We will bless the eight dollars and multiply them, just as Jesus multiplied the loaves and fishes." He taught that everyone has the power to bless and multiply, to heal and to prosper.

"What should I do now?" she asked.

I replied, "Follow your intuition. Do you have a 'hunch' to do anything or go anywhere?" **Intuition** means being taught from within. It is your unfailing guide.

The woman said, "I have a hunch to go home, but I only have enough money for the bus fare." Her home was in a distant city filled with lack and limitation. Her logical mind would have told her to stay in New York and find a job.

"Then go home," I said. "Never ignore a hunch."

I spoke these words for her: *"Infinite Spirit, open the way for great abundance for this woman. She is an irresistible magnet for all that belongs to her by divine right."* I told her to repeat this statement constantly.

She left for home right away. While visiting someone there, she reconnected with an old friend of her family. Through this friend, she received thousands of dollars in a way that felt like a miracle. She often tells me, "Tell people about the woman who came to you with eight dollars and a hunch."

There is always plenty waiting for you on your path. But it can only show up in your life through **desire**, **faith**, or the **spoken word**. Jesus made it clear that we must make the first move: "Ask, and it shall begiven you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you."

***

### The Only Enemy is Fear

The only things that stand between you and your highest ideals are **doubt** and **fear**. When you can "wish without worrying," your every desire will be instantly fulfilled.

Fear is the only real enemy. We experience:
* Fear of lack
* Fear of failure
* Fear of sickness
* Fear of loss

Fear is just **inverted faith**. It's having faith in a bad outcome instead of a good one.

The object of the game of life is to see your good clearly and to erase all mental pictures of evil. You do this by impressing your subconscious mind with a feeling and a vision of good.

A highly successful man told me he erased all fear from his mind after reading a sign in a room. The sign said: **"Why worry? It will probably never happen."** Those words stamped themselves on his subconscious mind. He now has a deep conviction that only good can come into his life—and so, only good appears.

***

### Creating a New Reality

You have a silent listener with you at all times: your subconscious mind. Every thought and every word is impressed upon it and carried out in amazing detail. It's like a sensitive recording device. If a singer coughs while making a record, the cough is recorded, too.

So, let's break all the old, bad records in our subconscious—the patterns in our lives we no longer want to keep. It's time to make new and beautiful ones.

Speak these words aloud with power and conviction:

**"I now smash and demolish every untrue record in my subconscious mind. They came from my own negative imagination, and they will now return to the dust. Through the power within me, I now make my perfect records: the records of Health, Wealth, Love, and Perfect Self-Expression."**

This is the square of life, the game completed. Any person who doesn't know the power of the word is behind the times. As the proverb says: "Death and life are in the power of the tongue."

## Chapter 2: The Law of Prosperity

One of the greatest spiritual messages is that a higher power, God, is your unlimited source of supply. You can release everything that belongs to you by divine right simply through your **spoken word**.

To do this, however, you must have complete faith in what you say. The prophet Isaiah said that his word would always accomplish what it was sent to do. We now understand that our words and thoughts are a powerful energy, a vibrational force that is constantly shaping our bodies and our lives.

***

### Act As If You Already Have It

A woman once came to me in great distress. She was going to be sued for $3,000 on the fifteenth of the month. She had no way to get the money and was in despair.

I told her that God was her supply and that for every demand, there is a supply. So, I "spoke the word" for her. I gave thanks that she would receive the $3,000 at the right time and in the right way. I explained that her job was to have **perfect faith** and, more importantly, to **act on that faith**.

The fifteenth of the month arrived, but the money hadn't shown up. She called me, panicked, asking what to do.

I told her, "It's Saturday, so they won't sue you today. Your part is to act rich. This will show that you have perfect faith that the money will arrive by Monday." She asked me to have lunch with her to keep her spirits up.

When we met at the restaurant, I said, "This is no time to be cheap. Order an expensive lunch. Act as if you have already received the $3,000."

The rule is, "Whatever you ask for in prayer, believing, you shall receive." You must act as if it has already happened.

The next morning, she called and asked me to stay with her all day. I said, "No. You are divinely protected, and God is never too late."

That evening, she called again, this time incredibly excited. "A miracle happened!" she said. "I was sitting in my room this morning when the doorbell rang. I told my maid not to let anyone in. But the maid looked out the window and said, 'It's your cousin, the one with the long white beard!'"

The woman told the maid to call him back. He was just turning the corner when he heard the maid's voice. He came back and talked for about an hour. As he was leaving, he said, "Oh, by the way, how are your finances?"

She told him she needed money, and he replied, "My dear, I will give you three thousand dollars on the first of the month."

She then said to me, "What do I do? I won't get it until the first, and I need it tomorrow!"

I told her I would keep "treating"—meaning, I would continue to affirm the positive outcome. I said, "Spirit is never too late. I give thanks that she has received the money on the invisible plane and that it will appear on time."

The next morning, her cousin called her. He said, "Come to my office this morning, and I will give you the money." That afternoon, she had $3,000 in her bank account.

***

### Prepare for Success, Not Failure

If you ask for success but prepare for failure, you will get the situation you prepared for.

For example, a man asked me to speak the word that a certain debt of his would be wiped out. But I found out he was spending all his time planning what he would say to the person he owed money to when he failed to pay. By doing this, he was canceling out my positive words. He should have been picturing himself paying the debt with gratitude.

***

### Dig Your Ditches

A wonderful illustration of this is in the Bible. Three kings were stuck in the desert, and their men and horses had no water. They consulted the prophet Elisha, who gave them a shocking message: "Thus says the Lord—You will not see wind or rain, yet you must make this valley full of ditches."

**You must prepare for the thing you have asked for, even when there isn't the slightest sign of it in sight.**

Here is a modern example. A woman needed to find an apartment in New York during a massive housing shortage. Her friends all felt sorry for her, saying, "Isn't it too bad you'll have to store your furniture and live in a hotel?"

She replied, "You don't need to feel sorry for me. I am a spiritual superman, and I will get an apartment." She affirmed: *"Infinite Spirit, open the way for the right apartment."*

She knew that for every demand, there is a supply. She was working on the spiritual plane, where there are no limits.

She was thinking about buying some new blankets when her logical mind—the "tempter"—whispered, "Don't buy the blankets. What if you don't get an apartment? You'll have no use for them."

She immediately replied to that negative thought, **"I'll dig my ditches by buying the blankets!"** She prepared for the apartment, acting as if she already had it.

In a way that felt like a miracle, she found the perfect apartment. It was given to her even though there were over 200 other applicants. The blankets were a symbol of her **active faith**.

And yes, the ditches dug by the three kings in the desert were filled to overflowing.

***

### Facing Your Doubts

Getting into this spiritual flow isn't easy for most people. Negative thoughts of doubt and fear rise up from the subconscious mind. They are like an "army of aliens" that you must defeat.

This explains why it is often **"darkest before the dawn."** A major success is usually preceded by waves of tormenting, negative thoughts. When you make a powerful spiritual statement, you challenge all the old, limiting beliefs in your subconscious. This is when you must repeat your affirmations of truth over and over. Rejoice and give thanks that you have already received what you asked for.

You can only receive what you can **see yourself receiving**. The Children of Israel were told they could have all the land they could see. This is true for every one of us. You only possess the "land" that exists within your own mental vision.

Often, right before a huge achievement, we are faced with apparent failure and discouragement. When the Israelites finally reached the Promised Land, they were afraid to enter. They said it was filled with giants who made them feel as small as grasshoppers. This is an experience almost everyone has.

However, a person who knows spiritual law is not disturbed by appearances. They rejoice even while things look bad. They hold on to their vision and give thanks that the goal is already accomplished.

***

### Hold the Vision

Jesus gave a wonderful example of this. He once said to his disciples, "Don't you say, 'There are still four months until the harvest'? I tell you, open your eyes and look at the fields! They are ripe already for harvest."

His clear vision pierced through the world of physical matter and saw the world as it truly is: perfect and complete in the Divine Mind.

So, you must always hold the vision of your journey's end. Demand the manifestation of that which you have already received, whether it is perfect health, love, money, or self-expression. These are all finished and perfect ideas in your superconscious mind. They are meant to come **through you**, not *to* you.

* **For example:** A man came to me asking for help with his business. He absolutely had to raise $50,000 by a certain date. The deadline was almost up, and he was in despair. The bank had flatly refused his loan.

I said, "I suppose you lost your temper at the bank, and therefore you lost your power. You can control any situation if you first control yourself. Go back to the bank, and I will 'treat' for you."

My affirmation for him was: *"You are identified in love with the spirit of everyone connected with the bank. Let the divine idea come out of this situation."*

He replied, "Woman, you're talking about an impossibility! Tomorrow is Saturday, the bank closes at noon, my train won't get me there until ten, and the deadline is tomorrow! It's too late."

I replied, "God doesn't need any time and is never too late. With Him, all things are possible. I don't know anything about business, but I know all about God."

He lived in a distant city. A week later, a letter arrived. It read: "You were right. I raised the money. I will never again doubt the truth of what you told me."

When I saw him later, I asked what happened. He said, "My train was late. I walked into the bank just fifteen minutes before it closed. I quietly said, 'I have come for the loan,' and they gave it to me without a single question."

In this case, the man couldn't have achieved this alone. He needed someone to help him **hold the vision**. Jesus knew the truth of this when he said, "If two of you shall agree on earth about anything they ask for, it will be done for them by my Father in heaven."

We often get too close to our own problems and become filled with doubt. A friend or a "healer" can see the success clearly because they aren't emotionally tangled in the situation. It's much easier to manifest something for someone else than for yourself. Don't hesitate to ask for help if you feel yourself wavering.

A keen observer of life once said, **"No man can fail, if one person sees him successful."** Such is the power of the vision. Many great people owed their success to a spouse, a sibling, or a friend who believed in them and held, without wavering, to the perfect pattern of their success.

## Chapter 3: The Power of the Word

When you understand the true power of your words, you become very careful about what you say. You only have to watch the results of your words to know that they always create an effect. Through your spoken word, you are constantly making laws for yourself.

***

### You Create Your Own Reality with Words

I knew a man who always used to say, "I always miss the bus. It pulls away right as I get there." His daughter, on the other hand, would say, "I always catch my bus. It's sure to come right when I need it."

For years, this is exactly what happened for both of them. Each one had created a separate law for themselves—one of failure, and one of success.

This is also the principle behind **superstitions**. A horseshoe or a rabbit's foot has no power in itself. But your belief and spoken word that it will bring you good luck creates a feeling of **expectancy** in your subconscious mind. This positive expectation then attracts a lucky situation to you.

***

### Superstition and "Lucky Charms"

However, as you grow spiritually and learn about this higher law, you'll find that relying on lucky charms no longer works. You can't turn back once you know the truth, and you must put away these kinds of idols.

* **For example:** Two men in my class were having great success in their businesses for several months. Then suddenly, everything fell apart. We tried to figure out what had changed. I discovered that instead of speaking their affirmations and looking to a higher power for their success, they had each bought a "lucky monkey" and were putting their faith in the objects.

I told them, "I see the problem. You've been trusting in lucky monkeys instead of God." I advised them to call on the law of forgiveness to neutralize their mistake. Man has the power to forgive and correct his errors. They decided to throw the lucky monkeys away, and their businesses immediately started doing well again.

This doesn't mean you have to throw away every lucky object you own. The key is to recognize that the **one and only power** is the Spirit *behind* the object. The object itself simply helps you feel a sense of positive expectancy.

* **For example:** I was with a friend who was in deep despair. As we crossed the street, she picked up a horseshoe. Instantly, she was filled with joy and hope. She felt that God had sent her the horseshoe as a sign to keep her courage up. At that moment, her hope became faith, and she eventually created a wonderful new reality for herself.

The difference is clear: The men were depending on the monkeys alone. The woman recognized the higher power *behind* the horseshoe.

I know from my own experience that it took a long time to break a belief that a certain event always brought me disappointment. The only way I could change this subconscious belief was to declare, **"There aren't two powers, there is only one power, God. Therefore, there are no disappointments, and this event now means a happy surprise."** I noticed a change right away, and happy surprises began to come my way.

***

### Facing Your Fears Defeats Them

A friend of mine told me that nothing could make her walk under a ladder. I explained to her, "If you're afraid, you're believing in two powers: Good and Evil. But since God, or Good, is the only real power, there can be no opposing power unless we create it with our own false beliefs. To show you believe in only One Power, you should walk under the next ladder you see."

Soon after, she went to her bank to open her safe-deposit box. A large ladder was standing right in her path. It was impossible to get to her box without going under it. She froze in fear and turned back. But as she reached the street, my words echoed in her mind. She decided to go back and face her fear.

It was a huge moment for her, as this fear had held her in bondage for years. She walked back to the vault, and the ladder was gone!

This is what so often happens. **If you become willing to do the thing you are afraid to do, you often don't have to do it.** This is the law of **nonresistance**. Courage truly contains genius and magic. When you face a situation fearlessly, the situation itself often disappears. It falls away under its own weight.

Fear attracted the ladder to her path. Fearlessness removed it. The invisible forces of the universe are always working for us, and we are the ones "pulling the strings" with our thoughts and beliefs, even if we don't realize it.

***

### Your Words are Magnets

Because of the vibrational power of words, whatever you say out loud, you begin to attract. People who constantly talk about being sick will invariably attract sickness. Once you know this truth, you can't be too careful with your words.

* **For example:** I have a friend who often calls me and says, "Come over and let's have a good old-fashioned chat." I know that this "chat" means an hour of hundreds of destructive words, mostly about loss, failure, and sickness.

I now reply, "No, thank you. I've had enough old-fashioned chats in my life; they're too expensive. But I would love to have a **new-fashioned chat** with you, where we talk about what we *want*, not what we don't want."

***

### The Golden Rule of Speech

There's an old saying that a person should only use their words for three purposes: **to heal, to bless, or to prosper.**

* What you say about others will eventually be said about you.
* What you wish for another person, you are also wishing for yourself.

A curse is like a boomerang; it always comes back to the sender. If you wish someone bad luck, you are guaranteeing that you will attract bad luck. If you help someone succeed, you are helping yourself succeed.

***

### Heal Your Mind to Heal Your Body

Your body can be renewed and transformed through your spoken words and clear vision. Disease can be completely wiped out of your mind and body.

A spiritual teacher knows that all disease has a **mental equivalent**. To heal the body, you must first "heal the soul." The soul is your subconscious mind, and it must be "saved" from wrong thinking. When the 23rd Psalm says, "He restoreth my soul," it means the subconscious mind must be restored with the right ideas.

The "mystical marriage" is the union of the soul (the subconscious) and the spirit (the superconscious mind). When your subconscious mind is flooded with the perfect ideas from your superconscious mind, you become one with the realm of perfect ideas. You become the person you were made to be, with power over your own mind, body, and life.

***

### The Ultimate Healer: The Law of Love

It is safe to say that **all sickness and unhappiness come from violating the law of love.** The great commandment is to "love one another." In the game of life, love and goodwill win every time.

* **For example:** A woman I knew had a terrible skin disease for years, which doctors called incurable. She was an actress and feared she would have to quit her job. One night, she had a huge success in a new role and received amazing reviews. The next day, she was fired. A jealous man in the cast had gotten her dismissed.

She felt overcome with hatred and resentment. She cried out, "Oh God, please don't let me hate that man." That night, and for the next two nights, she spent hours in silent meditation. She said she finally reached a deep state of peace with herself, with the man, and with the entire world. On the third day, she discovered her skin disease was completely gone.

Her disease came from subconscious resentment. By asking for the ability to feel love and goodwill, she fulfilled the law of love, and the disease was wiped out.

* Continual criticism can lead to rheumatism.
* Jealousy, hatred, and unforgiveness can cause tumors and false growths.
* Unforgiveness is one of the most common causes of disease. It can harden arteries, damage the liver, and affect eyesight.

I once told a woman who was sick from a "poisoned oyster," that the oyster was harmless; *she* had poisoned the oyster with her own thoughts. When I asked her "Who's the matter with you?" she admitted she had just quarreled with nineteen different people. Her inner conflict was so great that she attracted a bad experience.

As it is within, so it is without. Your only enemies are within yourself.

***

### Bless Your Enemies to Protect Yourself

This planet is currently taking its great lesson in love. Your real work is always with yourself. The goal is to perfect yourself in your interactions with your neighbor by sending goodwill and blessings to everyone.

The amazing thing is that if you truly bless someone, they have no power to harm you.

* **For example:** A man came to me for help with his business. He was selling machinery, and a rival appeared on the scene, claiming to have a better machine. My friend was afraid he would lose the sale.

I told him, "First, we must wipe out all fear. Know that your interests are protected and that the right machine will be sold by the right person to the right person. Do not hold a single critical thought toward that man. Bless him all day, and be willing to lose the sale if it isn't the divine plan."

He went to the sales meeting feeling fearless, nonresistant, and actively blessing his rival. He said the result was remarkable. The other man's machine refused to work, and my friend sold his machine without any trouble at all.

Love your enemies, bless those who curse you, and do good to those who hate you. Goodwill creates a powerful aura of protection around you. No weapon formed against you can succeed.

In other words, when you use love and goodwill to destroy the enemies within yourself—like hate, fear, and resentment—you will find you have no enemies on the outside.

There is peace on earth for the person who sends goodwill to all.

## Chapter 4: The Law of Nonresistance

Nothing on earth can stand in the way of a person who is completely **nonresistant**. 🌊

The ancient Chinese said that water is the most powerful element because it is perfectly nonresistant. It can wear away a solid rock and sweep everything before it.

Jesus Christ taught us to "resist not evil." He knew that in ultimate reality, there is no evil, so there is nothing to resist.

***

### Where Does "Evil" Come From?

The idea of "evil" comes from our own "vain imagination." It is a belief in two opposing powers, good and evil, instead of just one power: God, or Good.

An old legend says that Adam and Eve ate from "Maya, the Tree of Illusion." This caused them to see two powers instead of one. Therefore, evil is a false law that humans created for themselves. This happened through what could be called a "soul sleep"—a state where our souls (our subconscious minds) have been hypnotized by the collective beliefs of humanity in things like sin, sickness, and death. Our lives then become a reflection of these illusions.

As we've learned, your soul is your subconscious mind. Whatever you feel deeply, whether good or bad, is brought to life by this faithful servant. Your body and your life circumstances simply show what you have been picturing in your mind.

* A sick person has been picturing sickness.
* A poor person has been picturing poverty.
* A rich person has been picturing wealth.

This shows that your **Superconscious Mind** (the Christ or God within you) is the part of you that fights your battles and relieves you of your burdens. Therefore, you are violating a spiritual law if you carry a burden.

A **burden** is any negative thought or condition, and it always has its root in your subconscious mind. It's almost impossible to change the subconscious by giving it orders from your conscious, reasoning mind. Your intellect is limited and filled with doubts and fears.

So, the most scientific thing to do is to hand the burden over to your Superconscious Mind, where it is "made light" or dissolved into nothing.

As she handed it over to her Superconscious—which knows only of plenty—an avalanche of supply came to her.

* **For a negative emotion:** I knew a woman whose burden was resentment. For years, this feeling had held her in a state of torment. ### The Law of Use: Don't Hoard Your Blessings

All unhappiness and sickness come from violating the law of love. Your boomerangs of hate, resentment, and criticism always come back to you as sickness and sorrow. Love seems to be a lost art, but if you know spiritual law, you know it must be regained.

When I first started teaching, a student came to me month after month, trying to clear her consciousness of resentment. After a while, she was only resentful toward one woman. But that one woman kept her busy. Little by little, my student became more calm and harmonious. One day, all her resentment was finally gone. She came to me, radiant, and exclaimed, "You can't imagine how I feel! The woman said something to me, and instead of getting furious, I was loving and kind. She immediately apologized and was lovely to me! I feel a marvelous lightness inside."

Love and goodwill are also invaluable in business. When you "salute the divinity" in a difficult employer or colleague, you will be amazed at how they change.

***

### Let Go of Resentment and Regret

People are sometimes filled with remorse for something unkind they did years ago. If you can't go back and fix the wrong, you can neutralize its effect by doing a kindness for someone in the present. As the Bible says, "Forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before."

Sorrow, regret, and remorse tear down the cells of your body and poison your personal atmosphere. Don't brag about your troubles, or you'll always have something to brag about.

***

### Perfect Love Casts Out Fear

The old idea was that if a mother didn't worry about her children, she wasn't a good mother. We now know that a mother's fear is responsible for many of the diseases and accidents that happen to her children. Fear creates vivid pictures of the thing being feared, and these pictures become reality if they aren't neutralized.

Happy is the mother who can sincerely say that she puts her child in God's hands, knowing he is divinely protected.

* **For example:** A woman woke up suddenly in the middle of the night with a terrible feeling that her brother was in great danger. Instead of giving in to fear, she began making statements of truth: "Man is a perfect idea in Divine Mind and is always in his right place. Therefore, my brother is in his right place and is divinely protected." The next day, she found out that her brother had been right next to an explosion in a mine but had miraculously escaped unharmed.

In this way, you are your brother's keeper (in your thoughts). You should know that the people and things you love dwell "in the secret place of the Most High and abide under the shadow of the Almighty." If you know this, "There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling."

**"Perfect love casts out fear,"** and **"Love is the fulfilling of the Law."**

## Chapter 8: Intuition and Guidance

There is nothing you can't accomplish when you know the power of your words and follow your intuitive leads. With your words, you set unseen forces in motion. You can rebuild your body and reshape your life.

It's extremely important, therefore, to choose the right words. A student of this wisdom carefully selects the affirmation they want to send out into the invisible world. They know that God is their supply, that there is a supply for every demand, and that their spoken word releases this supply.

***

### How to Ask for and Receive Guidance

I have often been asked exactly how to make something happen, or "make a demonstration." My reply is always the same:

1.  **Speak the word.** Make your affirmation clearly and with faith.
2.  **Wait for a definite lead.** Don't do anything until you get clear inner guidance.

You should ask for this guidance by saying, **"Infinite Spirit, reveal to me the way. Let me know if there is anything for me to do."**

The answer will come. It might be through your intuition (a hunch), a random remark from someone, or a passage you read in a book. The answers are sometimes startling in their accuracy.

***

### The First Step: Giving Opens the Way

A woman wanted to receive a large sum of money. She spoke these words: *"Infinite Spirit, open the way for my immediate supply. Let all that is mine by divine right now reach me in great avalanches of abundance."* Then she added, *"Give me a definite lead. Let me know if there is anything for me to do."*

The thought came to her quickly and clearly: "Give a certain friend (who had helped her spiritually) one hundred dollars."

This was a very clear lead. Giving is what opens the way for receiving. To create activity in your finances, you should give. The old custom of **tithing**, or giving one-tenth of your income, is a sure way to increase your wealth. The tenth part you give goes out and returns to you blessed and multiplied.

But any gift or tithe must be given with love and cheerfulness. Bills should also be paid cheerfully. All money should be sent out fearlessly and with a blessing. This attitude makes you the master of money. Your spoken word can then open vast reservoirs of wealth. Your own limited vision is the only thing that limits your supply.

Sometimes a person has a great vision of wealth but is afraid to act on it. Your vision and your actions must go hand-in-hand.

***

### Your Inner Compass: Following Your Hunches

When you ask for guidance, never ask for just "a position" or "a house." Ask for the *right* position or the *right* house—the one that is already planned for you in the Divine Mind. That is the only one that will bring you true satisfaction.

* **For example:** A woman asked me to speak the word for a position. Soon after, she was offered three different jobs: two in New York and one in Palm Beach. She didn't know which one to choose, so she asked for a definite lead. One morning she called me and said, "When I woke up this morning, I could smell Palm Beach!" She had been there before and knew its unique, balmy scent. I told her, "Well, if you can smell Palm Beach from here, that is definitely your lead." She took the position, and it turned out to be a great success.

Your intuition is a spiritual faculty. **It doesn't explain itself; it simply points the way.**

Often, a lead will come to you at an unexpected time. One day, I was walking down the street and felt a strong urge to go to a bakery a few blocks away. My logical mind resisted, arguing that there was nothing I wanted there. But I have learned not to reason with my intuition. I went to the bakery, and sure enough, there was nothing there I wanted to buy. But as I was leaving, I ran into a woman I had been thinking of who was in desperate need of help that I could provide.

People have little idea how much their external affairs affect their bodies. There is a mental cause for every disease. A person's health problems can often come from a place of inner "congestion"—like hoarding things, hating someone, or holding onto fear. When you clear out the clutter in your life, you often clear up the problems in your body.

***

### Beware of Negative Thoughts and Outside Opinions

Jesus Christ knew that all sickness came from "sin" (which simply means violating a spiritual law). After healing a leper, he warned him to "go and sin no more, lest a worse thing come upon you." For a healing to be permanent, your soul (your subconscious mind) must be washed clean of negative thinking.

Many people attract disease and unhappiness by condemning others. **What you condemn in another, you attract to yourself.**

* **For example:** A friend came to me, angry because her husband had left her for another woman. She constantly condemned the other woman. I told her, "Stop condemning her and bless her instead. Otherwise, you are attracting the same situation to yourself." She wouldn't listen. A year or two later, she herself became deeply involved with a married man.

When you criticize or condemn, you are picking up a spiritual live wire. You should expect to get a shock.

If you struggle with **indecision**, repeat this statement often: **"I am always under direct inspiration. I make right decisions, quickly."** These words will impress your subconscious, and soon you will find yourself making the right moves without hesitation.

I have found it very destructive to look to the psychic world (like mediums or fortune-tellers) for guidance. That is the plane of *many minds*, not the "One Mind" of Infinite Intelligence. It's a world of opposites where you can receive good or bad messages. Things like numerology and horoscopes only deal with your Karmic path and can keep you stuck in negative expectations.

If someone gives you a prophecy of evil, you should immediately neutralize it with this statement: *"Every false prophecy shall come to naught. Every plan my Father in heaven has not planned shall be dissolved. The divine idea now comes to pass."*

However, if you receive a good message about coming happiness or wealth, you should hold onto it and expect it. The law of expectancy will help bring it into your life.

***

### The Right Way to Use Your Willpower

Your personal will should be used for one purpose: to back the universal will. Your statement should be: **"I will that the will of God be done."**

It is God's will to give you every righteous desire of your heart. Your willpower should be used to hold the perfect vision, without wavering. It is often much easier for people to have fear than to have faith. Therefore, **faith is an effort of the will.**

When you become spiritually awake, you recognize that any problem on the outside corresponds to a problem on the inside. If you physically stumble or fall, you should realize that you are also stumbling or falling in your thoughts.

***

### Trust the Process: How "Bad" Things Can Lead to Good

When you make your demands upon the Universal, you must be ready for surprises. Everything may seem to be going wrong, when in reality, it is all going right.

* **A remarkable example:** A woman had loaned $2,000 to a relative who died without leaving any record of the debt. For years, the woman was resentful. After learning spiritual law, she decided to collect the money from the "Bank of the Universal."

    First, she had to forgive the relative completely, because resentment and unforgiveness close the doors of this wonderful bank. Then, she made this statement: *"I deny loss. There is no loss in the Divine Mind; therefore, I cannot lose the two thousand dollars, which belong to me by divine right. As one door shuts, another door opens."*

    Soon after, an apparent injustice occurred. She lived in an apartment building that was being sold. Her landlord broke her lease and raised the rent. But this time, she was not disturbed. She blessed the landlord and said, "Since the rent has been raised, it just means I'll be that much richer, because God is my supply."

    Due to a mistake on the new leases, the landlord had to offer the tenants money to move out so he could sell the building. At first, he offered the woman $200. Then he offered her $1,500. A thought flashed in her mind: "Here comes the two thousand dollars!" She consulted with her neighbors, who advised her to hold out for more. The landlord agreed to pay her $2,000 to give up the apartment.

The apparent injustice was merely the door opening for her to receive what was hers. This proves that there is no loss. When you take your spiritual stand, you will receive all that is yours from the great Reservoir of Good. The universe can even restore to you "the years the locusts have eaten."

Negative thoughts are the only things that rob you. **"No man gives to himself but himself, and no man takes away from himself, but himself."** You are here to prove God and to bear witness to the truth. You can only do that by bringing plenty out of lack and justice out of injustice.

## Chapter 9: Your Divine Design

For every person, there is a perfect form of self-expression. There is a place you are meant to fill that no one else can fill. There is something you are meant to do that no one else can do. This is your **destiny**.

***

### Finding Your Life's Perfect Plan

This achievement already exists as a perfect idea in the Divine Mind, just waiting for you to recognize it. Since your imagination is your creative faculty, you must first be able to *see* the idea before it can become a reality. Therefore, the highest demand you can make is for the **Divine Design** of your life.

You might not have the faintest idea what your destiny is. There could be a marvelous talent hidden deep within you. Your request should be:

> *"Infinite Spirit, open the way for the Divine Design of my life to manifest. Let the genius within me now be released. Let me see the perfect plan clearly."*

The perfect plan includes health, wealth, love, and perfect self-expression. This is the **"square of life,"** and it brings perfect happiness.

***

### What to Expect When You Ask

When you make this demand, you may find that great changes start happening in your life. This is because most of us have wandered far from our true path. I know of one woman who, after making this demand, felt as though a cyclone had hit her life. But the old conditions were quickly replaced by new and wonderful ones.

**Perfect self-expression will never feel like hard labor.** It will be so absorbing and interesting that it will feel almost like play. You should also know that since you came into this world financed by God, the supply you need for your purpose will always be at hand. Many geniuses have struggled for years with money problems, when their spoken word and faith could have quickly released the necessary funds.

* **For example:** After class one day, a man handed me a single penny. He said, "I have just seven cents in the world, and I'm going to give you one. I have faith in the power of your spoken word. I want you to speak the word for my perfect self-expression and prosperity." I spoke the word for him and didn't see him again for a whole year. One day, he came to my class, looking successful and happy, with a thick roll of money in his pocket. He told me, "Right after you spoke the word for me, I was offered a job in a distant city. I am now living a life of health, happiness, and abundance."

A woman's perfect self-expression might be to become a perfect wife, mother, and homemaker, not necessarily to have a public career. The key is to ask for definite leads, and the way will be made easy and successful.

You shouldn't try to force a mental picture. When you ask for the Divine Design to enter your conscious mind, you will receive flashes of inspiration. You will begin to see yourself achieving something great. This is the picture you must hold onto without wavering. **The thing you are seeking is also seeking you.** The telephone was seeking Alexander Graham Bell!

Parents should never try to force careers on their children. By knowing this spiritual truth, they can speak the word for their child's Divine Plan early in life, or even before the child is born.

***

### Don't Rely on Your Own Power

There is a terrible penalty to be paid for not using your abilities. Often, **fear** is the only thing that stands between you and your perfect self-expression. Stage fright, for example, has held back many a genius. This fear can be overcome by the spoken word. The person then loses all self-consciousness and feels like they are simply a channel for Infinite Intelligence. They feel fearless and confident because they know it is the "Father within" who is doing the work.

* **For example:** A young boy in my class asked me to speak the word for his upcoming school exams. I told him to make this statement: *"I am one with Infinite Intelligence. I know everything I should know on this subject."* He knew his history well but wasn't confident in his arithmetic. He told me later, "I spoke the word for my arithmetic test and passed with the highest honors. But I thought I could depend on myself for history, and I got a very poor grade."

You will often have a setback when you are "too sure of yourself," which means you are trusting in your personality and not in your higher power. You must form the habit of "practicing the Presence of God" every single minute. "In all your ways acknowledge Him." Nothing is too small or too great.

***

### Let Go of Control

A student often holds back their success through resistance, or by trying to "point the way." They pin their faith on only one possible channel and try to dictate the exact way they want their desire to come true. This brings everything to a standstill.

The command of Infinite Intelligence is, **"My way, not your way!"**

Like electricity, spiritual power must have a nonresistant instrument to work through. You are that instrument. Over and over, we are told to "stand still." When you are poised and undisturbed, you give God-Power a chance to rush through you and accomplish its good pleasure.

When you are poised, you think clearly and make right decisions quickly. Anger, on the other hand, blurs your vision, poisons your blood, and causes you to make wrong decisions that lead to failure. This is why fear and worry are considered deadly sins—they are just inverted faith that brings about the very thing you fear. Your work is to drive these enemies out of your subconscious mind.

***

### Keys to Unlocking Your Design

Here are some important keys to manifesting your Divine Design:

* **Conquer Fear by Facing It.** The only way to get rid of fear is to walk right up to the thing you're afraid of. As soon as you are willing to do the thing you fear, you often find you don't have to. The situation vanishes.
* **Finish What You Start.** A student's financial success wasn't appearing. I suggested that perhaps she had a habit of leaving things unfinished, and her subconscious had gotten into the habit of not completing things. She went home and finished a sewing project she had started weeks ago. Shortly after, the money came in a very unusual way.
* **Be a Good Receiver.** Some people are cheerful givers but bad receivers. They block their channels by refusing gifts out of pride or some other negative reason. You must gracefully accept the good that comes back to you. All gifts are from God; people are just the channel. The Lord loves a cheerful receiver just as much as a cheerful giver.

***

### Why Are We Born into Different Circumstances?

People often ask why one person is born rich and healthy, while another is born poor and sick. The answer lies in the **Law of Reincarnation**.

You live through many births and deaths until you finally learn the truth that sets you free. You are drawn back to the earth plane to pay your Karmic debts or to "fulfill your destiny." A person born rich and healthy had pictures of health and riches in their subconscious mind in a past life. A person born poor and sick had pictures of disease and poverty. On any plane of existence, you manifest the sum total of your subconscious beliefs.

***

### Fulfilling Your Destiny

The real, spiritual you is birthless and deathless. You were never born, and you will never die. Through knowing the truth, you can be set free from the law of Karma, sin, and death.

Your freedom comes from **fulfilling your destiny**—bringing the Divine Design of your life into reality. When you do this, your Lord will say to you: "Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a few things; I will make you ruler over many things. Enter into the joy of your Lord."

## Chapter 10: Words of Power

The scripture says, "You shall decree a thing, and it shall be established for you." This means that what you declare with your words will become your reality.

All the good that is meant for your life already exists as a completed fact in the Divine Mind. It is released into your experience through your recognition of it, or through your **spoken word**. This is why you must be so careful to decree that only the Divine Idea becomes real, because your careless "idle words" can just as easily decree failure or misfortune.

***

### The Art of Asking Correctly

It is incredibly important to word your demands correctly. If you desire a home, a friend, a job, or any other good thing, you should always make the demand for the **"divine selection."**

For example:
> *"Infinite Spirit, open the way for my **right** home, my **right** friend, my **right** position. I give thanks that it now manifests under grace in a perfect way."*

The last part of that statement—"under grace in a perfect way"—is the most important part.

* **For example:** I knew a woman who demanded one thousand dollars. Soon after, her daughter was injured, and they received a one-thousand-dollar insurance payment. The money came, but it did not come in a perfect way.

Her demand should have been worded like this: *"Infinite Spirit, I give thanks that the one thousand dollars, which is mine by divine right, is now released and reaches me under grace in a perfect way."*

***

### Think Big: Expand Your Expectations

It is impossible for you to release more than you think is possible. You are bound by the limited expectations of your subconscious mind. You must **enlarge your expectations** in order to receive in a larger way.

* **For example:** A student of mine made the demand for six hundred dollars by a certain date. He received it, but he heard later that he had come very close to receiving one thousand dollars. He was given just six hundred, because that was the limit of his spoken word. People too often "limit the Holy One of Israel."

Wealth is a matter of consciousness. A French legend illustrates this perfectly.

A poor man was walking down a road when he met a traveler. The traveler stopped him and said, "My good friend, I see you are poor. Take this gold nugget, sell it, and you will be rich for the rest of your life."

The man was overjoyed. He took the nugget home but became so inspired that he immediately found work. He became so prosperous that he never got around to selling the nugget. Years passed, and he became a very rich man. One day, he met another poor man on the road. He stopped him and said, "My good friend, I will give you this gold nugget. If you sell it, it will make you rich for life."

The second man took the nugget, had it valued, and discovered it was only a worthless piece of brass.

The first man became rich because he *felt* rich, thinking the nugget was gold. **Every person has a gold nugget within them; it is your consciousness of gold, of abundance, that brings riches into your life.**

***

### The Feeling is the Secret

When you make your demands, you must begin at your journey's end. This means you must declare that you have *already received*. "Before you call, I shall answer."

Continually affirming this establishes the belief in your subconscious mind. It wouldn't be necessary to make an affirmation more than once if you had perfect faith! You should not plead or beg. Instead, you should **give thanks repeatedly that you have already received.**

This feeling of rejoicing, even while you are still "in the desert" of your old reality, is what opens the way for your good to be released. The Lord's Prayer is a series of commands and demands ("Give us this day our daily bread..."), and it ends in praise and thanksgiving ("For thine is the Kingdom and the Power and the Glory..."). Prayer, then, is a combination of command, demand, praise, and thanksgiving.

Your main work is to make yourself believe that **"with God, all things are possible."**

***

### The Bridge of Active Faith

This is easy to say in theory, but a little harder when you're actually facing a problem. You must do something to get a **realization** of your goal, because realization is what leads to manifestation.

* **For example:** A woman needed to receive a large sum of money by a specific time. She knew she had to do something to make it feel real, so she asked for a "lead." While walking through a department store, she saw a beautiful pink enamel paper-cutter and felt drawn to it. The thought came to her, "I don't have a paper-cutter nice enough to open letters containing large checks."

Her logical mind would have called this an extravagance. But she bought the paper-cutter. When she held it in her hand, she had a flash of inspiration—a clear picture of herself opening an envelope with a large check inside. In a few weeks, she received the money. The pink paper-cutter was her **bridge of active faith**.

***

### The Power of Your Mind

There are many stories about the power of the subconscious mind when it is directed by faith.

* **For example:** A man was spending the night in a farmhouse. The windows in his room had been nailed shut. In the middle of the night, he felt like he was suffocating. He made his way to the window in the dark but couldn't open it. So, he smashed the glass with his fist, took in deep breaths of what he thought was fresh air, and had a wonderful night's sleep. The next morning, he discovered that he had smashed the glass door of a bookcase. The window had remained closed all night. He had supplied himself with oxygen simply through his *thought* of oxygen.

***

### Final Keys to Winning the Game

When you start to demonstrate something, you must never turn back. The person who wavers will not receive anything.

* **Don't compromise.** A student of mine once said, "When I ask the Father for anything, I put my foot down and I say: 'Father, I'll take nothing less than I've asked for, but more!'"
* **Let go.** Demonstrations often come at the "eleventh hour," because that is when you finally stop reasoning and let go, giving Infinite Intelligence a chance to work.
* **Be patient.** Your impatient desires will either be long delayed or "violently fulfilled." For example, a woman who always said "I wish I could get rid of my glasses!" was constantly losing or breaking them. Her impatient desire was fulfilled literally. She should have been demanding perfect eyesight instead.
* **Follow denials with affirmations.** Always deny the negative appearance and affirm the spiritual truth. For example: *"I deny this appearance of disease. It is unreal and cannot register in his consciousness. This man is a perfect idea in the Divine Mind, pure substance expressing perfection."*
* **Trust visioning over visualizing.** Visualizing is a mental process, governed by your reasoning mind. **Visioning** is a spiritual process. It means receiving flashes of inspiration from your Superconscious mind and then working to make those divine pictures a reality.

Many people are building a bungalow in their imagination when they should be building a palace. If you desire only what God desires for you, the Master Architect will give you the true blueprints for your life.

All power is given to you through right thinking to bring your own heaven to your own earth. This is the goal of the Game of Life.

The simple rules are:
1.  **Fearless Faith**
2.  **Nonresistance**
3.  **Love**

Be transformed by the renewing of your mind.
